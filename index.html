Our Story

Maison Éclat was founded on a simple belief: that everyday luxury should feel personal, not performative. Each fragrance is composed in small batches, and every pair of sandals is finished by hand — because craftsmanship, not volume, is what makes something worth keeping.

From our atelier to your doorstep, every detail is considered: the weight of the glass, the stitch of the leather, the moment you first catch the scent in the air.
